# Content licensing and copyright checklist

**Do not assume that because a jazz tune is a standard, its composition, arrangement, score, recording and educational synchronization are public domain.** These are separate rights and can vary by territory and use. This document is a checklist, not legal advice.

For every asset record: source, owner, performer consent, composition/arrangement author, audio recording rights, sheet music/MIDI transcription source, public performance/sync/mechanical rights as relevant, territories, expiry, commercial use allowance, AI processing permission and attribution requirements. Obtain qualified counsel for launch territories.

- Your own recorded videos: confirm presenter/instructor, production company, session musicians and music excerpts were cleared for interactive online paid use and third-party AI processing/transcription.
- Public-domain works: check **composition** and individual arrangements/recordings separately; public-domain status not universal across territories.
- Commercial jazz standards: license melody/lead sheet/interactive rendering/playback and any backing recording/arrangements where required; do not scrape sheet-music sites.
- Upload workflow blocks publication if required rights fields absent/expired; flagged assets removed upon expiry.
- Original mascot/trademark: commission or generate an original design; avoid copying Bluey or a recognized character. Product name "JazzVerse Piano" provisional pending trademark and naming clearance.
- User-submitted practice recordings: explicit consent, clear retention/deletion, explain whether AI vendors process audio or transcription.
